# Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

A data analytics student project (SapphireIQ) analysing Supermart grocery sales, with findings on sales, products, profit, discounts and locations.

## Report
[Open the full project report (PDF)](Supermart_Grocery_Sales_Dashboard_Project_Report.pdf)

## Dataset
9,994 orders from 2015 to 2018, covering 7 categories, 23 sub categories, 24 cities and 4 main regions.

## Objectives
1. Understand overall sales performance
2. Understand product performance
3. Understand profit and discount
4. Understand geographic performance

## Key Results
- Total sales: Rs 1,49,56,982
- Total profit: Rs 37,47,121
- Profit margin: 25.05%
- Sales grew every year from 2015 to 2018, with peaks in September, November and December
- West and East bring about 60% of sales
- Health Drinks and Soft Drinks are the top sub categories
- Discount level shows no relationship with profit or order value
- Kanyakumari is the top city by sales

## Tools
Python (pandas, matplotlib) for analysis, and a PDF report. The interactive dashboard can be built in Power BI or Excel.
